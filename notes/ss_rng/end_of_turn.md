# End of turn effects

There are a handful of effects that occur at the end of the turn, such as poison damage, burn damage, etc. Most of these do not affect RNG or our pathing, but there ARE some. Since they can occur at the same time, it is important we get the order right.

## End of turn effects in order

1. Rampaging Status ends (from moves like Thrash)- Roll for confusion duration
1. "Drowsy" status putting pokemon to sleep, roll for duration
1. Future Attack Status ends (from moves like Future Sight) - Roll to hit
