# Ideal Metronome User

The ideal metronome user has lots of HP and defense to survive Tackles from the higher level Magikarp (including critical hits!), but low offensive stats so metronome doesn't faint Magikarp before you determine seed.

## Best stat spreads:

Impish Nature: +Def, -Sp. Atk
0 IVs for Atk and Sp.Atk
31 IVs for HP and Def
0-100 EV Investment for HP and DEF

## Current Favorite

Our current favorite is Level 7 Chansey: It's damage is _pretty_ low, its high base hp reduces risk of recoil faints, and outside of recoil or explosion it would take a max attack magikarp almost maximum possible rolls on two crits in order to faint Chansey before it gets off a second metronome.

This is the only pokemon we are going to support until P3, unless some edge case is revealed.

# All possible metronome users

## Cleffa, Clefairy, Clefable
Abilties: Magic Guard, Cute Charm

Pros:
* Cute Charm can introduce extra observable random rolls
* Magic Guard prevents recoils
* Decent defense, decently bad offense

Cons:
* Cute Charm does complicate things a bit
* Metronome best as egg move

Helpful moves to prevent:
* Fling
* Healing Wish

Recommendation: Lvl 5 cleffa

## Togepi, Togetic, Togekiss
Abilities: Hustle, Serene grace

Pros:
* Good defense
* Low offense
* Learns Metronome at lvl 6, the ideal level anyways
* Low speed - With 0 speed IV, will guarantee underspeed even Lvl 3 Magikarp, and Lvl 2 Magikarp without a speed lowering nature.
* Can learn Fling by TM

Cons:
* Hustle increases miss chance, which can be nice, but the additional phys. damage is probably not worth the risk. Consistency is best.

Helpful moves to prevent:
* Fling

Recommendation: Lvl 6 Togepi with 64 HP/92 Def investment
* Max Tackle Damage: 26-34.7%
* Atk/Sp.Atk: 7/8

## Happiny, Chansey, Blissey
Abilities: Natural Cure, Serene Grace

Always female

Pros:
* Natural Cure for pretty free ability
* Low offensive stats, esp. attack
* High HP, esp. Chansey/Blissey
* Chansey only breeds Happiny if you use Luck Incense

Cons:
* Low defense - But for Chansey/Blissey, High HP makes up difference
* Metronome as egg move only

Moves:
* Fling
* Healing Wish (Tricky)
* Thunder?
* Thunderbolt?
* Hyper Beam?
* Solar Beam?

Recommendation: Lvl 7 Chansey with 48 HP/68 Def investment
* 20 Max Atk Magikarp Tackle: 20-25.4%
* Atk/Sp.Atk: 5/8


## Snubbull, Granbull
Abilities: Intimidate, Run Away (Snubbull), Quick Feet (Granball)

* Bold Nature instead of Impish

Pros:
* Intimidate can lower Magikarp's attack

Cons:
* Metronome as egg move only
* High attack
* Kinda mediocre defensive stats outside of intimidate

Moves:
* Fling
* Roar

## Mew
Abilities: Synchronize

Pros:
* Can learn "Transform" to prevent transform shenaningans
* Good defensive stats 
* Synchronize has 50% chance to force magikarp to have matching nature.

Cons:
* Difficult to attain, esp. at low level.
* Good offensive stats
* Giving Magikarp Synchronize can cause issues

Moves:
* Fling
* Roar
* Self-Destruct
